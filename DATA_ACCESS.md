# KTZ Live data path

## Verified

- KTZ's [published service catalogue](https://bilet.railways.kz/post/schedule?_locale=ru) lists domestic, private and foreign-operated routes and is labelled with a month. It is a route catalogue, not a dated trip feed; train numbers can differ between it and station boards (for example 6803 and 803А), so number-only coverage comparisons are unreliable.
- The map's separate railway-station layer comes from named `railway=station` and `railway=halt` nodes in the [Geofabrik Kazakhstan OpenStreetMap extract](https://download.geofabrik.de/asia/kazakhstan.html), under ODbL. It can include freight locations, duplicates and border areas, and cannot establish that every station is present.
- The KTZ [station renovation list](https://bilet.railways.kz/post/trainnews1009) is useful for finding missing passenger facilities, but is not a complete passenger-station register. The added boards were matched to KTZ station-search IDs, OSM coordinates, and at least two plausible matched timetable segments. This is a consistency check, not independent proof that every coordinate and trip is correct.
- Public stop-by-stop timetables do exist: Yandex lists [train 077Т Алматы–Мангистау](https://rasp.yandex.kz/thread/R_077T_111?departure_from=2026-09-08+04%3A18%3A00&station_from=9619416) with 44 stops, although that particular indexed version is dated September 2026. [Advantour](https://www.advantour.com/rus/kazakhstan/trains/timetable.htm) publishes a route index; several displayed durations are inconsistent with the displayed day offsets, so it is a discovery source, not a verified current trip inventory. Yandex's API terms restrict processing and altering data; neither source has been established as a redistributable nationwide feed.
- Kaspi's rail-ticket service was launched through a direct KTZ–Kaspi IT integration, not a documented anonymous public feed: https://rail-news.kz/kz/passenger-transportation/9052-poyyz-biletterin-endi-kaspi-kz-zh-ktemesi-ar-yly-satyp-alu-a-bolady.html
- KTZ's computing centre publishes an information-services contract and application, and lists a marketing contact: https://iservice.railways.kz/ . Its public description refers to rolling-stock location; whether it covers passenger-train GPS and third-party map publication needs confirmation.
- The official tracking site's robots notice disallows automated `/api/` collection and republication: https://tablo-railways.kz/robots.txt . Do not use its internal endpoints for this hosted map without written access.
- The Kazakhstan open-data portal has a published rail routes/schedules dataset (`list_and_schedule_of_socially_`, version `v18`) but requires a developer key: https://data.egov.kz/datasets/view?index=list_and_schedule_of_socially_ . Check its fields and license before relying on it for dated trips.
- Yandex Timetables has a documented schedule/stop API, but its terms restrict processing and altering API data. An estimated-position map would require specific permission: https://yandex.ru/legal/rasp_api/ru .

## Dated stop-feed investigation, 15 November 2026

- **Yandex Timetables is a concrete candidate, not yet a usable map feed.** The [station-schedule method](https://yandex.ru/dev/rasp/doc/ru/reference/schedule-on-station) returns dated train and suburban departures and journey `uid`s. The [thread method](https://yandex.ru/dev/rasp/doc/ru/reference/list-stations-route) takes a `uid` and date and returns intermediate stations and times. A visible international example is [007Р Алматы–Саратов](https://rasp.yandex.kz/thread/R_007R_111?departure_from=2026-11-01+20%3A27%3A00&station_from=9619528&station_to=9619444). Nationwide coverage, cancellations, request quota, and full international coverage have not been tested. [Registration](https://yandex.ru/dev/rasp/doc/ru/concepts/access) is required for an API key, but [terms 2.3.3 and 3.2.4](https://yandex.ru/legal/rasp_api/ru/) restrict processing, alteration and storage, making written authorization necessary to confirm our estimated-position use. A key alone does not settle that question.
- **KTZ ticketing integration is a second, partner-only candidate.** [Nemo.travel's KTZ documentation](https://support.nemo.travel/ru/%D0%9A%D0%A2%D0%96) describes KTZ contracts, test credentials and certificates for an agent, and its Nemo Connect API. This documentation was last edited in 2021; current requirements, train/stop coverage, schedule-only access, pricing, and rights to publish derived map positions must be confirmed with KTZ or the provider.
- **1TR.KZ is another possible partner, not a verified feed.** [Its site](https://1tr.kz/) advertises rail and bus ticket integration and lists an agency contact. It does not establish that a public map may ingest dated rail stops or estimated locations. The site lists bpm@mobportal.kz and +7 747 842 30 59 for agencies.
- **Freedom Travel publishes detailed timetables**, but its [robots.txt](https://freedom-travel.kz/robots.txt) disallows automated route-detail paths. Its [advertised business API](https://freedom-travel.kz/business/api) explicitly covers aviation, so it must not be represented as a rail-feed solution.

The existing 100-board scraper remains partial. The monthly KTZ list is a route catalogue, not proof that a particular dated trip operates; station count is not trip coverage. The 2015 independent [open-data survey](https://global.census.okfn.org/entry/kz/timetables.html) is historical background, not proof of today's availability.

Suggested request to the passenger-data owner at KensePP@railways.kz (listed on [KTZ's contact page](https://railways.kz/ru/contacts)) or a rail-data partner:

> Просим сообщить, возможно ли получить тестовый и промышленный машиночитаемый канал действующего расписания всех пассажирских поездов, проходящих по Казахстану, включая пригородные, международные и транзитные рейсы. Нужны идентификатор рейса и дата отправления, признак отмены, все остановки с датами, временем и часовыми поясами, периодичность, координаты станций и частота обновления. Просим указать охват перевозчиков, стоимость, лимиты и условия хранения, расчета приблизительного положения по расписанию и публичного отображения на бесплатной карте. Если доступен GPS, просим отдельно указать условия доступа и публикации координат и время обновления.

## Access request to KTZ

Ask the Passenger Transportation operator and KTZ Computing Centre for (1) a dated passenger train trip feed with train number, unique trip ID, start/end and intermediate stop timestamps; (2) GPS positions where available, timestamps and update interval; (3) coverage and service-level expectations; (4) rate limits, price, attribution and permission to cache, estimate missing positions and publish a free Kazakhstan-wide map. Request a test feed and written reuse terms. KTZ Computing Centre lists IT-services marketing contact `nurgali_ae@railways.kz`; verify the correct passenger-data owner before contracting.

## Build once access is confirmed

Ingest only the licensed feed, normalize times to UTC, deduplicate by trip ID and service date, reject stale GPS, and calculate estimated positions between adjacent stops with the C++ core. Follow rail geometry, not straight lines, before describing locations as geographically precise. Publish source, timestamp, freshness and uncertainty per marker. Run a coverage check against the operator's trip inventory; never claim “all active trains” until that check passes.
